Clarified New York Milk Sour
Difficulty: Expert

Servings 3 Cocktails
Ingredients
3 oz Bourbon
2 oz Freshly Squeezed Lemon
1 oz Orgeat Syrup
5 dashes Angostura Bitters
2 oz Taylor Tawny 10 yr Port
30 mL Cold 2% Milk
30 mL Cold 35% Cream

Instructions
In a mixing jar, combine bourbon, lemon, orgeat syrup, bitters and port. 
Stir with 1 ice cube.

In a separate container, mix milk and cream and pour the 
cocktail into the milk mixture. 
Give this a quick stir before putting into the fridge. Cover.
Leave this for at least 1 hour in the fridge. 

Filter

Recipe Notes
Note that the preservation method with clarified milk allows you to 
keep a cocktail when kept refrigerated for 
upwards of a few months to a year
 (although I would give it just a month to be safe).
