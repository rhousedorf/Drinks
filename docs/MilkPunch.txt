


Boston Sour Milk:
Build in Shaker
Add
1 oz or 30 ml Lemon Juice
3 oz or 90 ml Whiskey
1 oz or 30 ml Oleo Saccharum
3 oz or 90 ml Whole Milk
shake
Strain through a coffee filter or vacuum filter

Pour back into shaker
Add
1 Egg White
Dry Shake
Add ice and shake again
Strain into glass
Garnish with Angostura Bitters
