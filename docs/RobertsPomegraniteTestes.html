Roberts' Pomegranite teste drink recipes.

Bourbon teste #1

1.5 Oz bourbon
3 Oz of your preferred pomegranite jooce.
1/3 Oz of Orange Curacao
Ice as you see fit.
Water if you want to dilute it down before the ice melts.
YOU will likely NEED to adjust the ratio of Bourbon to granite jooce, to get the most pleaseing flavour for your palatelet.

Gin teste #1

3 Oz cheap London Dry gin, Burnett's in this case. '
PLASTIC Green bottle! w00t!
1 Oz of your preferred pomegranite jooce.
3/4 Oz of Orange Curacao
4 dashes Fee's Lime Bitters
4 dashes Fee's West Indian Orange Bitters
3/4 Oz Lime juice, from a Lime,
or Real Lime also works

Ice as you see fit.
Water if you want to dilute it down before the ice melts.
Adjust as needed to get the most pleaseing flavour for your palatelet.
