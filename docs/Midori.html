Midori recipes.

Midori Sour #2:

1 oz. or 30 ml. Lemon Juice
1 Egg White
.5 oz or 15 ml. Simple Syrup
1.5 oz. or 45 ml. Midori
1 oz. or 30 ml. Vodka
Dry Shake
Add ice and shake again
Strain into glass
Garnish with Angostura Bitters (Optional)

Midori Sour #4:


1 oz. or 30 ml. Lemon Juice
2 oz. or 60 ml. Midori
1.5 oz. or 45 ml. Whiskey
One Dash Angostura Bitters
One Dash Peychaud's Bitters
3 Dashes Angostura Chocolate Bitters
1 Egg White
Bar Spoon Simple Syrup
Add ice and shake
Strain into glass

.75 oz. or 22 ml. Lime Juice
.5 oz. or 15 ml. Chartreuse
1 oz. or 30 ml. Midori
