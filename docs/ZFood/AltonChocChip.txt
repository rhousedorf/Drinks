

227 grams (2 sticks) unsalted butter
340 grams bread flour
1 teaspoon kosher salt
1 teaspoon baking soda
60 grams granulated sugar
225 grams light brown sugar
1 large egg
1 large egg yolk
30 g / ml whole milk  over3g milk powder & 45 g water
8 g / ml vanilla extract
340 grams semisweet chocolate chips



Melt the butter in a 2-quart saucepan over low heat. 
SLIGHTLY Alton BROWN it!
Set aside to cool slightly.

Sift together the flour, salt, and baking soda.

Pour the butter into the bowl of your stand mixer. 
Add the sugars and 
beat with the paddle attachment on 
medium speed for 2 minutes.

Meanwhile in a separate bowl, whisk together the whole egg, egg yolk, milk, and vanilla extract.

Slow the mixer to “stir” and 
slowly work the egg mixture into the butter and sugar. 
Mix until thoroughly combined, about 30 seconds.
Using the paper plate as a slide, gradually integrate the dry ingredients, 
stopping a few times to scrape down the side of the bowl with a rubber spatula. 
Once the flour is worked in, 
add the chocolate chips
I'd suggest chopping your own dark chocolate chunks in the 68% cocoa range.. 
Chill the dough.

next day

Heat oven to 375°F and 
place the racks in the top third and bottom third of the oven. 
Line 4 half-sheet pans with parchment paper and set aside.
Scoop the dough into 1 1/2-ounce portions and 
place on the half-sheet pans, 6 cookies per sheet.
Bake two sheets at a time for 15 minutes, 
rotating pans halfway through baking.
Remove from the oven, 
slide the parchment with the cookies onto a cooling rack, and 
wait at least 5 minutes before devouring.
