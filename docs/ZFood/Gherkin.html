No, they do not use 100% pure spirit vinegar. The industrial brine is diluted with water to reach a precise, uniform total acidity level—typically around 5% to 6% acidity for Maille cornichons. ## Why Your Homemade Version is Overpowering Standard clear spirit vinegar (white vinegar) bought at grocery stores is already diluted to 5% acidity in the bottle. * If you used it straight from the bottle without adding water, your final brine was 5% total acidity. * While the Maille factory targets a similar final acidity, they pack the jars so tightly with raw cucumbers that the internal water inside the cucumbers leaks out and dilutes the vinegar even further down to around 2% to 3% final acidity in the jar. * If you cooked your vinegar, evaporated water, or used a high-strength pickling vinegar (like 10% or 20% cleaning/industrial spirit vinegar), the acid punch will be overwhelmingly harsh. ## The French Factory Ratio (Calculated for Home Testing) To replicate the industrial Maille flavor balance without the overpowering burn, French food processors calculate the dilution based on the total weight of the cucumbers and liquid combined. For a standard home jar, the liquid ratio to mimic that commercial bite is: * 2 parts spirit vinegar (standard 5% white vinegar) * 1 part water * Salt (calculated at 3% to 4% of the total water/vinegar weight) This specific ratio accounts for the water that will naturally sweat out of the cucumbers, keeping the final crunch sharp without burning your throat.
