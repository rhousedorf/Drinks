


Sour Gherkins (Cornichons)
try 1, this sucked.

Ingredients
Saltwater Soak
2 tbsp canning salt
1 cup water
baby cucumbers

Canning Spices and Brine
peppercorns 3-5 per quarter pint jar
allspice berries 1-2 per quarter pint jar, crushed
mustard seed
1 cup vinegar White or Cider or Champagne or White Wine
1 tbsp canning salt

Instructions
Make a brine with 
1 part salt and 
8 parts water, or 
2 tablespoons of salt to each cup of water.  

Soak the tiny cucumbers for a few hours or overnight.

Drain the baby cucumbers and pack them into canning jars with spices.  
I've used 5 peppercorns and 2 allspice berries to each quarter pint canning jar.
