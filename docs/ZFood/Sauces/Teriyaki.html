Teriyaki Sauce

Jen's Teriyaki

1/2 C tamari -or- Pearl River Bridge, Golden Label ( FUCK my dick its good) Superior Light Soy Sauce.
Yes, fuck you, that oughta be the name.
1/4 cup brown sugar
IF YOU LIKE GINGER,
and not the Gilligans Island Ginger...
1 1/2 teaspoons fresh ginger ,minced

1 teaspoon garlic ,minced, ( 2 cloves, heavy for me )
(I skip this sweet) 1 tablespoon honey (Vegans: use agave nectar or sweetener of choice)
1 teaspoon sesame oil
1/4C mirin
Bring to quick boil, then simmer for 2 minutes, done.
OPTIONALLY, add 1/4 cup water mixed with 3 teaspoons cornstarch to make it thikk.
